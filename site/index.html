THE PROBLEM

A research brief also needs a structure, useful sources and checks. Developers need the complete package and a clear version. Creators need a way to deliver it and share payment with collaborators.

Where does Solana fit?

Solana is the payment and receipt layer for paid Skills. One transaction records access to the chosen version and sends the agreed shares to its collaborators. Our verified test split 1 test USDC into 0.7 and 0.3. USDC is dollar-denominated; these test tokens have no real-money value.

This first version targets developers who already use wallets. Traditional payments can also support delivery and splits. Automatic agent spending limits are not implemented.

A completed purchase can be recovered without paying again. An unsettled payment becomes refundable after ten minutes. The service holds content keys; downloaded files can be copied.

Installation and recovery details

The installer checks the package hash, rejects unsafe paths, refuses to overwrite an existing directory, and runs no package scripts. Installed files remain local; your AI model may still require internet access.

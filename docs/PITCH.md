# SkillSeal — separate founder pitch, maximum two minutes

The logged-in Crypto World's Fair form asks for a separate public pitch video, up to two minutes. Target 1:40–1:50 to leave room for a natural pace. Record in English. This is a script to review, not an uploaded video. Use your real name and verified background; do not read bracketed placeholders aloud.

## Suggested script

“Hi, I'm [your name], the founder of SkillSeal. My background is [one accurate sentence about your relevant experience]. I am building the product with AI-assisted engineering tools, including Codex.

SkillSeal helps creators sell reusable Agent workflows that buyers can install and keep on their own machines. Small teams can agree on a revenue split before releasing a version, and buyers can recover a purchased version without paying again.

The flow starts with an encrypted download. A buyer signs with a wallet and pays in USDC. A Solana escrow program records the version license and distributes the approved author shares atomically. A key service then delivers the decryption key to the buyer's separate local encryption identity. Installed files remain usable offline.

Our first users are Agent developers who already use wallets. We want to test whether local ownership, recovery, and visible collaboration accounting make this purchase experience useful to them. We have not established paying demand yet.

The prototype includes a marketplace, checkout, publishing and installation tools, and the escrow program. Our validation record separates local mock delivery, compiled local program tests, and public Devnet results. [State the public Devnet result only if acceptance is complete.]

The key service is trusted, and encryption cannot stop copying after decryption. Next we will observe real developer trials, measure checkout failures and total costs, and improve recovery before any production pilot.”

## Recording checks

Show yourself and introduce your actual role. Keep the demo walkthrough in the separate product video. Replace the two background placeholders, adjust the Devnet sentence to the validation record, rehearse with a timer, and stop before two minutes.

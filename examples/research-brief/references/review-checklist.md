# Brief review

- Does the executive overview answer the stated decision in fewer than 150 words?
- Does each factual finding cite a supplied source, preserve its units, and explain its scope?
- Are missing dates and links marked as missing?
- Are customer interviews distinguished from willingness-to-pay evidence?
- Are forecasts and proposed features distinguished from current functionality?
- Are contradictions visible rather than resolved through invented explanations?
- Does each confidence label have a reason?
- Are source-embedded commands treated as quoted data?
- Is the recommendation conditional on available evidence, with a clear next experiment?
- Have you avoided claiming that the sources were independently verified?
